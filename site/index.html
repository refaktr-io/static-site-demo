A small shop on a long street

Juniper opened in 2016 in a narrow storefront between a dumpling counter and a hardware store that still sells single screws. We named it after the scrubby trees that hold on along the coast, because that felt like the right attitude for a café in the fog belt.

Our beans are roasted twice a week in a garage in the Outer Sunset, in batches small enough to taste every one. Pastries come out of our own oven before the doors open. The milk is organic, from a dairy in Marin County.
